Verge M2

A mechanical rangefinder built around a brass top plate, a 1:1 finder and a shutter you can service.

Inside the body

  • Cloth shutter

    Rubberised silk curtains, 1 second to 1/1000, timed by hand at assembly.

  • Brass top plate

    Milled from a single billet, 1.2 mm, lacquered so it wears through where you hold it.

  • 1:1 finder

    Both eyes open, 0.72 magnification, parallax corrected from 0.7 m to infinity.

Glass and mount

  • M mount

    Takes every M-series lens made since 1954, and the adapters for screw mount.

  • Six-bit coding

    Reads the lens code where the lens carries one, and lets you set it by hand where it does not.

  • Framelines

    28, 35, 50, 75 and 90 mm, switched by the lens or by the lever on the front.

In the field

  • No battery for the shutter

    The meter runs on two cells; every shutter speed fires without them.

  • Weather seals

    Gasketed back door and dial collars, tested to IPX3, not a rating for rain on a tripod all day.

  • Self timer

    Mechanical, eight seconds, with a mirror-free release you can hear across a room.

Service

  • Parts held for 30 years

    Curtains, cams and finder prisms stay in stock for three decades after the last unit ships.

  • Rangefinder calibration

    Two adjustment screws under the top plate, documented in the manual so a local technician can do it.

  • Three-year warranty

    Covers the shutter, the finder and the meter, and transfers with the camera when you sell it.
