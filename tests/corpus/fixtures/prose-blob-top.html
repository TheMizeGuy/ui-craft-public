About the Thornfang Blade

The Thornfang Blade has been part of the Emberfall loot table since the Ashen Expanse patch, and players have argued about its place in the melee rotation ever since. Its base damage sits below the two other weapons that drop in the same wing, but the bleed it applies stacks with poison in a way the tooltip does not spell out, which is why parse sites keep ranking it higher than its raw numbers suggest.

Our figures come from combat logs uploaded over the last thirty days, filtered to encounters completed on the highest difficulty with a full group. Logs from practice attempts and from groups that wiped before the second phase are excluded, because partial fights weight the early burst window too heavily and make every bleed weapon look stronger than it plays across a whole encounter.

Drop rates are harder to pin down. The blade is a guaranteed drop from one chest and a chance drop from three others, and the chance rolls appear to share a single hidden counter rather than rolling independently, so a player who has opened all four in the same week sees a different rate than the community average printed below.

Enchant choices have shifted twice since launch. The community settled on sharpening early, moved to the bleed-duration enchant after the Ashen Expanse tuning pass, and has drifted back towards sharpening now that the poison cap is enforced server side.
