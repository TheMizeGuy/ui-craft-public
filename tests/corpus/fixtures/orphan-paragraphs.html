How balances are calculated

The open balance is the sum of every issued invoice minus payments received and credits applied, taken from the ledger rather than from the billing provider.

Disputed lines stay in the balance until the dispute closes. They are not written back out when a dispute is opened, because reversing them early made the monthly close harder to reconcile.

Payment terms

Standard terms are net thirty from the issue date. Enterprise agreements override the standard terms and are stored on the contract record, not on the customer record.

Late fees accrue daily but are only assessed when an invoice closes, so an invoice paid on day thirty-five carries five days of fees and nothing more.

Who to contact

Billing questions from customers go to the finance inbox. Questions about what this page shows go to the platform team, who own the ledger service behind it.

Escalations on anything in the collections queue go to the finance lead directly, and the platform team cannot change a collections record from here.
